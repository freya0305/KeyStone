# DECISION: Design Partner Program — Strategy & Scope

**Date:** 2026-05-10

## Decision

Design partner program is maintained as a **customer discovery and B2B reference asset**, NOT a subscription revenue driver. Scope is reduced from 50-100 users to **5-10 deep engagements** per cohort, prioritizing genuine product validation over volume.

**Value of design partners is NOT measured by subscription ROI** — it is measured by:

1. B2B reference customers for agency/university sales (written testimonials, call referrals)
2. Case study data for procurement (quantifiable outcome evidence)
3. Product validation signal (does the core use case actually work?)
4. Warm introductions to institutional buyers

If design partners cannot deliver the above, the program should be abandoned in favor of direct agency sales (2-4 week sales cycle, actual paying customers, stronger references).

## Rationale

Design partner subscription ROI is structurally negative on subscription revenue alone:

- 100 users × 6 months × SGD 12 = SGD 7,200 foregone revenue
- Plus 1:1 resume review (SGD 150 each) = SGD 15,000 value
- Total investment: SGD 22,200 per cohort
- At 10% conversion: SGD 1,440/year subscription return → payback period: 15+ years

The program survives only if it produces B2B reference assets (not subscription revenue). If it fails to produce those assets, reallocate founder time to direct agency sales instead.

## Scope Adjustment

| Original               | Revised                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 50-100 design partners | 5-10 deep design partners                               |
| 6 months free access   | 3 months free access                                    |
| 1:1 resume review      | Optional, only for highly engaged partners              |
| Broad enrollment       | Application-based (must demonstrate genuine use intent) |

**Why fewer but deeper**: 5 partners who genuinely use the product and produce measurable outcomes (testimonials, case studies, warm intros) are worth more than 50 partners with accounts that never activate.

## Success Criteria for Design Partner Cohort

Each design partner must produce at least ONE of the following to "complete" the engagement:

1. Written testimonial (2-3 sentences on specific outcome)
2. Referral call with a prospective B2B buyer
3. Case study data: X students used KeyStone, Y completed applications, Z% response rate improvement
4. Warm intro to career centre director or agency owner

Partners who do not produce any of the above after 3 months are not renewed.

## Capital Impact

Design partner program has negligible capital impact (forgone SGD 7,200 subscription revenue) but significant founder time cost (1:1 maintenance). Reducing to 5-10 partners frees founder time for agency sales, which produces actual revenue AND stronger references.

## Related Decisions

- B2B-first strategy: Agency sales (SGD 79-199/month) prioritized over university sales in Year 1
- University first contract target: SGD 15-20K (director-level approval, no VP procurement)
- Minimum capital: SGD 30K reserves required
