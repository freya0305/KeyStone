# Competitive Pricing Research — Recruitment JD Tools & AI Resume Services

## Competitor Pricing

### Textio (LinkedIn acquired 2023)

- **Model:** Enterprise SaaS, no public pricing
- **Est. Cost:** USD 100-200/seat/month (large enterprise)
- **Notes:** JD writing + pipeline intelligence; acquired by LinkedIn

### LinkedIn Talent Solutions

| Product        | Pricing                           |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Job Slots      | ~SGD 100-500/month (volume-based) |
| Recruiter Lite | ~SGD 120-200/month per seat       |
| Enterprise     | Custom (SGD 50K-500K+/year)       |

### Skillroads (AI Resume Builder)

- **Freemium:** Free tier available
- **Individual:** ~USD 19-49/month
- **Enterprise/B2B:** Custom quote

### Jobalytics (Resume Keyword Analyzer)

- **Freemium:** Free tier available
- **Paid:** ~USD 15-29/month

### HireVue (AI Assessments)

- **Essential:** Custom quote
- **Professional:** ~USD 150-250/user/month (estimated)
- **Premium:** USD 250-400+/user/month (estimated)

## KeyStone Agency Pricing vs Market

| Plan  | Current              | Market Range                   | Assessment              |
| ----- | -------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| Basic | SGD 49/mo            | SGD 167-667/seat/mo (JD tools) | **61-93% below market** |
| Pro   | SGD 129/mo           | SGD 167-667/seat/mo            | **51-81% below market** |
| Team  | SGD 299/mo (5 seats) | SGD 80-335/seat/mo             | **11-61% below market** |

**Conclusion:** KeyStone Agency pricing is significantly below market for a JD-specific AI tool. Even after raising to SGD 79/199/449, it would still be at or below mid-market.
