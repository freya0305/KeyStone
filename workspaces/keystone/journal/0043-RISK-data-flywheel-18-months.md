## Risk

The core moat (suggestion feedback data) takes 12-18 months to accumulate enough for meaningful AI differentiation.

### Timeline

| Month | Data Volume | AI Capability |
|-------|------------|---------------|
| Month 0-3 | 0-500 records | Generic advice (no moat) |
| Month 3-6 | 500-2,000 records | Basic RAG (directionally correct) |
| Month 6-12 | 2,000-5,000 records | Bandit ranking (measurably better) |
| Month 12-18 | 5,000-10,000 records | Predictive model (significant differentiation) |
| Month 18+ | 10,000+ | Compounding moat |

### The Window

Competitors (Teal, LinkedIn) can copy KeyStone's features in 4-8 weeks. The 12-18 month data accumulation period IS the actual moat — but only if KeyStone survives long enough to build it.

### Question

Does KeyStone have runway to survive 18 months without meaningful revenue from the data flywheel?
