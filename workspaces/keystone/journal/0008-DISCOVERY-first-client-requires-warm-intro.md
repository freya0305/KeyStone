## Discovery

Analysis of three directly comparable B2B-first career tech companies (VMock, Handshake, Forage) reveals a consistent pattern: **the first institutional client was always won through a warm network connection, never through cold outreach**.

- VMock → Northwestern Kellogg: founder was a Northwestern MBA alumnus
- Handshake → Michigan Tech: founders were current students
- Forage → Australian institutions: founders' home network

There is zero documented case of a first institutional client won on a cold email with no prior relationship in edtech B2B.

**The implication**: If the KeyStone founding team has no direct or second-degree connection to a SG university career centre, finding that connection is the highest-priority task — before the product, before the pitch deck, before any other GTM work.

## What "Warm Connection" Means in Practice

A warm connection is not necessarily someone who will become the client. It is anyone who can make an introduction to the relevant decision-maker (career centre director or their deputy) with a personal voucher — "I know this founder, they are credible, you should take 20 minutes."

Sources of warm connections for SG university career centres:
- NUS/NTU/SMU/SIT alumni who are currently career advisors or career centre staff
- Faculty at target institutions who have career-services involvement
- Career coaches or consultants who work with multiple institutions
- Other founders who have already pitched or worked with SG career centres
- Employment/HR professionals at major SG companies who serve on career advisory boards
- LinkedIn 2nd-degree connections to directors at the target institutions

## Recommended Pre-Outreach Action (Before Any Product Build)

The founding team should map their extended network for every person with any connection to SG university career services. This mapping exercise should happen in Week 1, not after the product is built.

If the mapping reveals zero connections, the next step is:
1. Attend SG career-focused events (NUS/NTU Career Fairs, Singapore HR Institute events, EnterpriseSG startup events where career centre directors are present)
2. LinkedIn message alumni network peers who work in HR or career advisory roles asking for introductions
3. Engage with SkillsFuture or EnterpriseSG ecosystem listings that give informal credibility before first contact
