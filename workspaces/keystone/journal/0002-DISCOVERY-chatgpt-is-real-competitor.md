# DISCOVERY: ChatGPT Is the Real Competitor, Not Teal or Jobscan

**Type**: DISCOVERY
**Phase**: 01-analyze
**Date**: 2026-04-29

## Finding

The brief benchmarks against Teal, Jobscan, LinkedIn, and Resume.io. These are the wrong competitors. The actual alternative a Singapore job seeker evaluates against KeyStone is ChatGPT (or Claude), used directly.

A SG job seeker with a 200-word system prompt — "You are a Singapore career coach. The resume is below. The JD is below. Rewrite each bullet to mirror GLC/MNC tone and SG conventions" — gets approximately 70% of KeyStone's output for free.

The remaining 30% that ChatGPT cannot replicate:
- URL parsing of MCF/JobStreet (no copy-paste friction)
- NS-aware framing rules (ChatGPT knows this only generically)
- GLC vs MNC company-type detection
- Structured accept/reject/modify UI per suggestion line
- Outcome tracking and personal callback rate

## Implication

KeyStone is priced at SGD 19/mo against ChatGPT Plus at USD 20/mo (~SGD 27/mo). KeyStone is cheaper but must be **visibly more useful per session for a SG job seeker** — not marginally better, but clearly better in ways the user can feel in the first 5 minutes.

If users don't perceive quality superiority over their own ChatGPT usage, the SGD 19/mo charge is unsustainable regardless of feature depth.

## Action Required

Before launch, run a blind comparison test: 20 SG job seekers (10 male fresh graduates, 10 mid-career switchers) get KeyStone output vs ChatGPT output for the same resume + job. Measure:
1. Perceived quality (which version would you use?)
2. NS framing quality (for male participants)
3. Job-specificity (did the suggestions reference the actual JD?)

If the blind test does not demonstrate a clear perceived quality advantage, the product is not ready to charge.
