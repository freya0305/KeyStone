## Decision: 8 Strategic Commitments Defining Success Path

This entry documents the 8 non-negotiable commitments that define whether the KeyStone model succeeds or fails.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Lean Operations Path
- **Option A**: Raise VC seed (SGD 500K+) — Rejected. Too early, too dilutive, shifts focus from product to fundraising.
- **Option B**: Personal funds + angel bridge — **Selected**. Maintains founder control and product focus.

### B2C Paid Acquisition
- **Option A**: Paid acquisition from Day 1 — Rejected. LTV (SGD 36) < CAC (SGD 40-80). Loses money on every user.
- **Option B**: Organic-only B2C — **Selected**. Referral program, career coach partnerships, Reddit, LinkedIn.

### University Revenue Timeline
- **Option A**: Target Year 1 university revenue — Rejected. 18-24 month procurement cycle makes this unrealistic.
- **Option B**: University outreach from Day 1, revenue Year 2-3 — **Selected**. Builds data for B2B pitch while B2C grows.

### Annual Plan Positioning
- **Option A**: Annual as "discount" — Rejected. Users won't prepay 12 months for a product they'll leave in 3.
- **Option B**: Annual as "Career Ecosystem Pass" — **Selected**. Targets post-hire users who want to stay tracked.

---

## The Model Viability Contract

**The model WORKS if**:
- [x] Founder accepts lean operations (personal funds OR angel bridge)
- [x] B2C growth is organic only (no paid acquisition)
- [x] Agency deals prioritized from Day 1
- [x] University outreach starts immediately
- [x] Government grant applied Month 1
- [x] Annual Plan positioned as post-hire ecosystem tool

**The model FAILS if**:
- [ ] Founder expects B2C subscription revenue to fund operations
- [ ] Paid acquisition attempted for B2C
- [ ] University timeline underestimated
- [ ] Government grants counted on without backup plan

## Consequences

| Commitment | Operational Impact |
|------------|-------------------|
| Lean operations | Monthly burn SGD 600-3.5K (vs industry SGD 10-20K) |
| Organic-only B2C | Slower user growth but sustainable unit economics |
| Agency priority | First revenue Month 2-3; bridges to university deals |
| University Day 1 | Relationship-building starts before competitors notice |
| Grant Month 1 | 6-12 week processing; cash runway must cover gap |
| Annual = Ecosystem | Post-hire target segment; different conversion trigger |
| B2B primary | Data moat strategy; B2C serves data collection |
| Break-even Month 12-24 | Honest timeline; no artificial acceleration |
