## Discovery: The LTV/CAC Math Has a Clear Verdict on Paid Acquisition

Financial model recalibration (SGD 19 → SGD 12) revealed something more important than the pricing correction: the LTV/CAC ratio tells us exactly when paid acquisition is viable.

### The Numbers That Emerged

| Metric | Monthly Pro | Annual Plan |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| Price | SGD 12/mo | SGD 144/yr |
| LTV | SGD 36 (3mo avg) | SGD 144 |
| CAC | SGD 40-80 | SGD 40-80 |
| Viable? | **No** | **Yes** |

### The Clear Verdict

- **Monthly Pro at SGD 12**: LTV (SGD 36) < CAC (SGD 40-80) → paid acquisition loses money on every user
- **Annual Plan at SGD 144**: LTV (SGD 144) > CAC (SGD 40-80) → paid acquisition can work

### The Strategic Insight: Annual Plan Is Not a Discount, It's the Paid Acquisition Enabler

The annual plan isn't just "a discount for committed users" — it's the only path to viable paid acquisition. This means:

1. **Growth strategy must lead with Annual Plan** for paid acquisition channels
2. **Monthly Pro is a conversion step, not an acquisition channel** — users start monthly, upgrade to annual when they see value
3. **B2C free + organic is the data engine** — free users build the outcome database that makes B2B pitches credible

### Recalibrated Year 1 Financial Targets

| Scenario | Pro Users | B2C ARR | Agency ARR | Total ARR |
|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|

### Break-Even Timeline

- Break-even at ~400 Pro users: Month 18-24
- This is honest about the ramp — B2B agency deals bridge Months 1-12 while B2C builds
