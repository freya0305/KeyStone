# Journal Entry

## DECISION: Annual Plan Cancelled — Only $12/month Pro Remains

**Slug**: annual-plan-cancelled-pro-only
**Type**: DECISION
**Date**: 2026-04-30

## Decision

Annual Plan ($144/year) is cancelled. Product will only have $12/month Pro tier.

## Rationale

- Singapore job search cycle: 2-4 months average
- Users who get a job stop using the product immediately
- Annual subscription model is fundamentally misaligned with job seeker behavior
- No one will prepay for 12 months of job search they don't need

## Updated Pricing

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Limited analyses |
| Pro | $12/month | Unlimited analyses, full suggestions, outcome tracking |

## Impact

- Simpler pricing = easier to explain, easier to convert
- No LTV extension from annual plan
- Revenue model relies entirely on monthly Pro subscriptions
- Focus shifts to: keeping users engaged post-hire for referral/data flywheel
