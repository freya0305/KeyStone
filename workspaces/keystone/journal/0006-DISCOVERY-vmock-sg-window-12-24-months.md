## Discovery

VMock, the most directly comparable B2B competitor, has no confirmed contracts with any Singapore university career centre as of August 2025 training cutoff. Named SG universities — NUS, NTU, SMU, SIT, SUSS, SUTD — are not listed in VMock's public partner materials.

VMock's stated strategic direction is US-first expansion, then UK/India, then broader Asia Pacific. The estimated timeline for VMock to close its first SG university contract, assuming they begin serious outreach now, is **18–36 months**.

This creates an actionable **12–24 month window** for KeyStone to establish institutional relationships in SG before VMock's Asia expansion becomes a direct threat.

A critical uncertainty: the absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. VMock may have undisclosed SG pilots. **The most important pre-sales intelligence task is direct verification**: LinkedIn searches for VMock employees + Singapore, direct outreach to SG career centre directors to ask what tools they currently use.

## Implications

1. KeyStone is not displacing an incumbent in SG — it is creating a new budget line at most institutions. This is meaningfully easier than displacement.
2. The correct framing for urgency in the B2B pitch is time-to-first-mover advantage: "Sign before VMock arrives and you lock in SG-native intelligence and outcome data they will never have." This is a legitimate urgency argument.
3. The window assumes VMock does not acquire or partner with a SG company in the interim. Monitor for VMock + SG EdTech acquisition signals.

## VMock's Core Vulnerability (Exploit in Pitch)

VMock is a document scoring tool universities buy for predictability and brand, not demonstrated outcome improvement. The fundamental gap: VMock **cannot tell a career director whether students who used it got better callback rates**. This is precisely the metric career directors must report to their Provost and Board.

KeyStone's outcome tracking capability is the structural answer to the single question VMock cannot answer. Every university conversation should be anchored to this.
