## RISK: 30% Conversion Rate Assumption Is 10x Industry Average

**Slug**: conversion-rate-assumption-overestimated
**Type**: RISK
**Date**: 2026-04-30

## Risk

Business model assumes 30% B2C conversion (free → Pro). Industry average for freemium SaaS is 2-5%.

### Impact on Break-Even

- **Assumption**: 143 Pro users needed for break-even = 477 registered users
- **Reality (5% conversion)**: Need 2,860 registered users = 6x more than assumed
- **Reality (2% conversion)**: Need 7,150 registered users = 16x more than assumed

### This Changes the Entire Business Model

If 30% → 5%:
- Monthly revenue: $3,600 → $600 (at 1000 registered users)
- Break-even: Not achievable in Year 1
- Burn: Not sustainable
