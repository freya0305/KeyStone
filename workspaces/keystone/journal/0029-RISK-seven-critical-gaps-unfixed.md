## The Seven Critical Gaps

### Gap 1: No Capital Runway Plan
Year 1 revenue (SGD 21-50K) < Year 1 burn (SGD 43-75K). Gap = SGD 0-54K.
**Question for founder**: Are you self-funding (personal cash), raising angel (SGD 300-500K), or applying for Startup SG Tech? Decision changes everything.

### Gap 2: EAA Opinion Letter Missing
Product cannot launch without clarity on whether resume suggestions = "employment advice" under Singapore Employment Act.
**Question for founder**: Have you engaged an employment lawyer? Budget SGD 3-8K. Timeline: Week 1-2.

### Gap 3: Single Founder Burnout
Month 1-3 workload = 60-80 hrs/week across B2C, B2B, compliance, product, marketing.
**Question for founder**: What are your non-negotiable tasks? What can be delegated or cut?

### Gap 4: No PDPA DPO Engaged
Cannot lawfully process resume data (personal data) without a DPO.
**Question for founder**: Which DPO provider? DPMS, AAGEN, or RHTMindSHIELD? Get quotes Week 1.

### Gap 5: Startup SG Tech Treated as Certainty
Grant may be rejected or delayed 12+ weeks.
**Question for founder**: What is your plan B if the grant doesn't come through?

### Gap 6: 2% Conversion Scenario Not Modeled
At 2% conversion: Year 1 B2C ARR = ~SGD 180-360. Burn = SGD 43K. Gap = SGD 42K+.
**Question for founder**: At what conversion rate do you pivot, cut costs, or raise emergency capital?

### Gap 7: Data Moat Too Slow
1,500 outcome records in 6-month window is not statistically significant.
**Question for founder**: Is the moat narrative "institutional relationships" (SIT, SMU as first-movers) or "raw data volume"? Choose one.
