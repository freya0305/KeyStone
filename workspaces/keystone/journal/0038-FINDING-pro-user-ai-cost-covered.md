## Finding

Researched market AI costs and calculated whether $12/month Pro pricing covers AI costs for a typical Singapore job seeker.

### Cost Breakdown Per Pro User Per Month

Based on 8 job analyses/month (moderate user):
- **Phase 1 (now)**: $1.04/user/month — 91% gross margin ✅
- **Phase 2 (6 months)**: $0.60/user/month — 95% gross margin ✅
- **Phase 3 (12 months)**: $0.23/user/month — 98% gross margin ✅

### Worst Case (Heavy Applier - 30 analyses/month)
- AI cost: $3.90/user/month
- Gross margin: 68% — still profitable ✅

### Key Conclusion

**AI cost is NOT the risk. CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) is the real risk.**

- $12/month Pro × 3 months avg subscription = $36 LTV
- CAC must be <$20 to break even on monthly users
- CAC >$50 = never profitable

### Singapore User Behavior Assumption

Moderate user: 8 analyses/month
Heavy applier (海投): 15-30 analyses/month
