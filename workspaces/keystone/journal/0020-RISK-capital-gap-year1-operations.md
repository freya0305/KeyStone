## Risk

The plan assumes KeyStone can reach break-even through organic B2C growth without external capital. The math does not support this:

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Monthly infrastructure burn | SGD 2.6-3K |
| Founder personal burn (SG) | SGD 3-5K/month |
| **Total monthly burn** | **SGD 5.6-8K** |
| Month 12 realistic MRR | SGD 1.5-4.6K |
| **Gap at Month 12** | **SGD 1-6.5K/month** |
| **Year 1 capital needed** | **SGD 60-90K** |

Realistic Year 2 ARR (university contracts in Year 3, not Year 2) is SGD 120-250K, not the SGD 328-756K projected.

## Why

- Organic ceiling: ~3-5K registered users/year → 120-300 Pro users → SGD 27K-68K ARR
- Break-even requires: SGD 100K ARR
- Gap: SGD 32-73K ARR that organic cannot close

## How to Apply

Before /todos, founder must answer: How is Year 1 funded? Personal savings? Startup SG grant (SGD 50K)? Angel investment? This decision changes the entire build strategy — if capital is tight, B2B pivot becomes urgent, not Phase 3.

## For Discussion

1. Is SGD 60-90K in personal savings + grants realistic for the founder?
2. Does the B2B pivot need to happen earlier if external capital is not available?
3. At what point does the plan become "build B2B first, B2C later" rather than "B2C to build the moat"?
