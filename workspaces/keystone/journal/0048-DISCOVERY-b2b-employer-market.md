**Date**: 2026-05-03

## Key Findings

### 1. Market Gap Exists
No direct competitor focuses specifically on "helping recruiters/agencies write better JDs fast." Current tools:
- Jobalytics: Job seeker side only
- Textio: Enterprise/inclusive hiring focus
- LinkedIn: Full recruitment platform (JD is minor feature)
- Template sites: No AI, no personalization

### 2. Recruitment Agencies Are Best First Target

**Why:**
- Pain point: Write 10-50 JDs/day manually
- Time value: 1 hour saved = $50-250 (based on typical commission)
- Decision maker: Recruiter can decide themselves (not HR head/CEO)
- Market gap: No dedicated tools for this segment

### 3. Pricing Logic

```
Value-based pricing derived from time savings:
- If tool saves 1-2 hours/day writing JDs
- 1 hour = $50-250 value (based on commission rates)
- Monthly value = $1,000-5,000
- So $49-129/month is reasonable

Recommendation:
- Agency Basic: $49/month (1 user, 50 JDs)
- Agency Pro: $129/month (1 user, unlimited JDs)
- Agency Team: $299/month (5 users)
```

### 4. Differentiation

KeyStone JD tool:
- NOT a resume matching platform (unlike LinkedIn)
- NOT inclusive hiring tool (unlike Textio)
- IS: AI-powered JD generator for recruiters/small businesses
- Based on: Learning from public resume data what successful candidates look like
- Output: JD that attracts the right candidates
