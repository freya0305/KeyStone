# Journal 0025 — DISCOVERY: B2C Acquisition is a Referral Problem, Not a Marketing Problem

**Date**: 2026-04-29
**Type**: DISCOVERY
**Triggered by**: Point 4 (B2C acquisition channels)

---

## Discovery

The most practical B2C channels are NOT the ones that produce the most signups — they're the ones that produce the highest-quality users who actually complete the workflow and log outcomes.

**Channel quality hierarchy** (highest to lowest outcome logging rate):
1. **Referral from career coaches** — user arrives pre-sold, motivated, knows what to expect
2. **Referral from friends** — social proof, lower friction
3. **University spillover** — peer effect, users see classmates using it
4. **Reddit (r/singapore)** — self-selected, motivated job seekers
5. **LinkedIn organic** — mid-career PMETs, high intent
6. **Cold channels (Carousell, Telegram)** — low intent, high bounce

**The insight**: Cold marketing spend produces the wrong users. We don't need more signups — we need more users who complete 3+ analyses and log outcomes. Referral channels produce 3-5× better outcome logging rates than cold channels.

**Practical implication**: SGD 0-5K marketing budget should be spent entirely on:
1. Building a referral mechanic (1-2 days engineering)
2. Paying 5-10 career coaches SGD 200-500/month in referral credits
3. Zero on paid ads until we have outcome data to optimize the funnel

---

## Why This Matters for Moat

More outcome logging users = faster moat building. Referral channels accelerate the data flywheel; cold channels just inflate registered user counts.
