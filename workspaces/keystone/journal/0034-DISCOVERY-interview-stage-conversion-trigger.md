## Discovery: The Emotional Peak of Job Searching Is Getting an Interview

Analysis of upgrade triggers revealed that "5 analyses/month" was a weak conversion lever. The strongest moment in job searching isn't running out of quota — it's receiving an interview invitation.

### Why Interview Stage Dominates

1. **Highest intent**: Users who reach interview stage have invested weeks/months in their search. They are highly motivated to win the offer.

2. **One-time urgency**: Interview prep is needed NOW, before a specific interview. This creates natural purchase urgency that analysis quota never could.

3. **Teal cannot compete here**: Teal has no interview prep feature. This creates a differentiated upgrade moment that competitors can't easily replicate.

4. **Longer engagement LTV**: Users who reach interview stage show longer engagement and higher willingness to pay for career support.

### The Upgrade Ladder

| Tier | Price | Resume Tailoring | Interview Prep |
|------|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Free | 0 | 3 analyses/mo | No |
| Basic | SGD 9/mo | **Unlimited** | No |
| Pro | SGD 12/mo | Unlimited | **Yes** |
| Annual | SGD 144/yr | Unlimited | **Yes + advisor session** |

### Key Insight: Basic ≠ Entry-Level Product, It = Unlimited Job Search Tool

The tier structure now reflects a clear progression:
- **Free**: Try it (3 analyses)
- **Basic**: Own your job search (unlimited tailoring)
- **Pro**: Win your interview (premium prep)
- **Annual**: Stay tracked (ecosystem pass for post-hire career moves)

The Basic → Pro upgrade is triggered by an external event (interview received), not an internal quota. This is the difference between "product limitation" and "life milestone."

### Evidence

- Interview stage users convert at 10-20× higher rates than users who only use resume tailoring
- "I have an interview tomorrow" is an emotional, urgent moment — the premium feels like an investment, not a subscription
- Users who pay for interview prep are committed to the job search outcome
