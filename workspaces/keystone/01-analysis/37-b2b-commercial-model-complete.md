## Executive Summary

KeyStone's B2B revenue architecture is the PRIMARY financial story of this company. B2C is a data-acquisition channel that feeds the B2B flywheel — it is not the end state. The path to break-even runs entirely through institutional B2B contracts, not consumer subscriptions. With the right segment sequencing and execution discipline, KeyStone can reach break-even on B2B revenue alone within 18-24 months, using B2C data to make the B2B pitch undeniable.

**Break-even requires**: ~3 university deals (SGD 15-30K each) OR ~10 agency deals (SGD 10K/year each), supplemented by ~200-300 active Pro users. This is achievable within Year 1 if agencies are prioritized first (2-4 week sales cycle) and university pilots are running in parallel.

## 1. B2B Revenue Architecture

### 1.1 University Career Centre Tiers

| Stage | Price | Scope | Sales Cycle | Decision Maker |
|-------|-------|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Pilot (free) | SGD 0 | 50-200 seats, 1 semester | 4-8 weeks | Career centre director |
| Year 1 Contract | SGD 15-30K | One programme or graduating class | 6-12 months | Procurement + VP approval |
| Year 2+ Contract | SGD 50-100K | Full career centre licence | 9-18 months | Provost + Procurement + PDPA audit |

**Procurement thresholds** (Singapore public universities):
- Below SGD 10K: career centre director approves directly
- SGD 10-50K: dean/VP sign-off + procurement office review
- Above SGD 50K: VP/Provost approval + Procurement Office + PDPA audit + vendor due diligence

**First university target**: NUS, NTU, SMU, or SUSS career centre. Position as "pilot expansion" (converting existing free pilot relationship into a paid contract) rather than a new vendor engagement. This cuts the sales cycle from 12-18 months to 4-8 months.

### 1.2 Recruitment Agency Tiers

| Tier | Price | Contract | Decision | Typical Agency Size |
|------|-------|----------|----------|---------------------|
| Starter | SGD 10/seat/month | Monthly, no minimum | Owner/director, 2-4 weeks | 1-5 recruiters |
| Growth | SGD 7/seat/month | Annual, 10+ seats | Same | 10-30 recruiters |
| Enterprise | SGD 5/seat/month | Annual, 50+ seats | Committee (rare) | 50+ recruiters |

**Agency deal economics** (illustrative):
- 5-recruiter agency at SGD 10/seat: SGD 600/year
- 20-recruiter agency at SGD 7/seat: SGD 1,680/year
- 50-recruiter agency at SGD 5/seat: SGD 3,000/year

**Unit economics per agency seat**: Cost SGD 1.90/seat/month (shared infrastructure) + ~SGD 0.50/seat/month support = SGD 2.40/seat/month total cost. At SGD 10/seat/month, gross margin per seat = SGD 7.60/month (76%).

### 1.3 Government (WSG) Tier — Year 2-3

| Stage | Price | Timeline | Notes |
|-------|-------|----------|-------|
| Initial engagement | SGD 0 | Year 2 | Grant-funded pilot or free pilot |
| GeBIZ tender (SGD 6K+) | SGD 30-80K/year | Year 2-3 | Competitive tender, 3 quotes required |

**Strategy for WSG**: Build product capability to serve PMET and Career Transition Programme (CCP) participants now (so the capability exists when the tender comes). Submit AI resume tools RFP if issued. Being the best-qualified Singapore-native vendor is the entire strategy for this channel.

### 1.4 Target Account Selection Criteria

**University priority (by ease of entry)**:
1. SUSS — most industry-aligned, fastest procurement
2. SMU — smallest, most agile decision-making
3. NTU — largest, longest cycle (worth the effort for reference account)
4. NUS — longest cycle, highest prestige

**Agency priority (by strategic fit)**:
1. Small boutique agencies (5-15 recruiters) — fastest decisions, founder relationships
2. Specialised vertical agencies (banking, tech, healthcare) — strongest outcome data signal
3. Major recruitment firms (Randstad, Robert Half) — Year 2 targets, long cycle

## 3. How B2C Supports B2B

### 3.1 The Data Flywheel

```
B2C Users (free + Pro)
        │
        ▼
  Application records
  (what jobs they target, how they phrase resume)
        │
        ▼
  Outcome data (hired / not hired + employer signals)
        │
        ▼
  Outcome-calibrated scoring
  ("users who followed KeyStone suggestions had 23% higher offer rate at Company X")
        │
        ▼
  B2B pitch evidence
  ("our data from 2,847 Singapore job seekers shows...")
        │
        ▼
  Faster B2B close rate
        │
        ▼
  More B2B contracts
        │
        ▼
  More employer partnerships
        │
        ▼
  More B2C data (employer feedba +referral)
```

**The flywheel is B2C's only job**. B2C revenue is secondary; B2C data is primary.

### 3.2 What B2C Data Actually Provides

| Data Type | B2C Accumulation | B2B Use |
|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| Application volume | 6-month head start on employer fingerprinting | Shows employer-specific acceptance patterns |
| Outcome tracking | Hired/not-hired + employer name (with consent) | Validates which suggestion patterns correlate with offers |
| Resume phrasing | SGD 0 cost to collect, millions of patterns | Fine-tuning corpus for resume analysis |
| Job market signals | Real-time supply/demand by role, by employer | Differentiates KeyStone from generic resume tools |
| Consent-chain | Explicit PDPA consent for outcome follow-up | Employers trust data that has a clean consent chain |

### 3.3 B2C Scale Requirements for B2B Credibility

A B2B pitch to a university career centre requires statistical credibility. Rough thresholds:
- **500+ outcome records** with verified outcomes: minimum for internal pilot discussion
- **2,000+ outcome records**: sufficient for a Year 1 university pitch deck
- **5,000+ outcome records** with employer cross-reference: strong enough to win competitive tender

At current B2C trajectory (Year 1: 3K-8K registered, 150-400 active Pro), the outcome data threshold for university credibility is reachable by Month 9-12 if conversion tracking is implemented from Day 1.

## 4. B2B Sales Execution Plan

### 4.1 Segment Priority: Agencies First, Universities in Parallel

**Why agencies first**:
- 2-4 week sales cycle (vs 6-18 months for universities)
- No procurement process for deals under SGD 10K
- Owner/director decision — no committee
- Immediate cash flow
- Reference accounts for university pitch ("we already serve 5 agencies including [Name]")

**Why universities in parallel** (even though slow):
- Pipeline moves slowly but produces large contracts (SGD 15-100K)
- Free pilot creates relationship before competitors enter
- University reference = credibility signal for agency sales
- Pilot to Year 1 contract conversion is the highest-value sales motion

**WSG is Year 2-3 only** — do not spend cycle time on government in Year 1.

### 4.2 Agency Sales Cycle (2-4 weeks)

```
Week 1: Outreach + Discovery
  - LinkedIn outreach to 20 agencies (target: boutique/specialised)
  - Book 3-5 discovery calls
  - Goal: understand pain point (candidate preparation quality, offer acceptance rate)

Week 2: Demo + Proposal
  - 30-minute demo: show candidate prep workflow
  - Customise pitch to their pain: "your candidates prepared with KeyStone → higher offer acceptance → you earn placement fee faster"
  - Send proposal with 3 seat tiers

Week 3: Follow-up + Close
  - Follow up on proposal
  - Address objections (price, implementation friction, data privacy)
  - Close or move to Month 2 pipeline

Week 4: Onboard + Expand
  - Send welcome email with setup instructions
  - Set up agency dashboard (see their candidates' prep status)
  - Identify expansion opportunity (more seats, annual plan)
```

**Agency conversion rate assumption**: 15-25% from discovery call to signed. Outreach to close: ~8-12%.

### 4.3 University Sales Cycle (6-18 months)

```
Phase 1: Pilot Pipeline (Month 1-3)
  - Identify 1-2 target universities
  - Intro call with career centre director
  - Propose free pilot (50-200 seats, 1 semester)
  - Set up data sharing agreement + PDPA consent framework

Phase 2: Pilot Execution (Month 4-9)
  - Run free pilot
  - Track outcomes: application submissions, offer rates, student satisfaction
  - Collect case study material ("N students used KeyStone, X% received offers at Y companies")

Phase 3: Year 1 Contract Expansion (Month 8-14)
  - Present pilot results to career centre director
  - Navigate procurement (VP sign-off for SGD 15-30K)
  - Negotiate contract terms

Phase 4: Year 2 Upgrade (Month 15-24)
  - Present Year 1 outcome data
  - Expand scope to full career centre
  - Navigate full procurement (Provost + Procurement + PDPA audit)
  - Close SGD 50-100K Year 2 contract
```

**Critical path**: The pilot-to-contract conversion depends entirely on outcome data quality. A pilot with tracked outcomes (who applied, who got offers, employer names) is 5x more likely to convert than a pilot with only usage metrics.

### 4.4 First 5 Concrete Actions

1. **Week 1**: Identify 20 target agencies on LinkedIn (specialised, 5-20 recruiters, Singapore-based). Send personalised outreach messages. Target: 5 discovery calls booked.

2. **Week 2**: Draft agency one-pager and proposal template. Set up Stripe for seat-based billing. Configure agency dashboard (per-agency candidate prep view).

3. **Week 3**: Identify 2 target university career centres (SUSS + one of NTU/SMU). Draft pilot proposal. Set up PDPA-compliant consent chain for outcome tracking.

4. **Month 2**: Launch agency outreach campaign (email + LinkedIn). Target 3 signed agency deals by end of Month 2. This provides cash flow AND first B2B reference accounts.

5. **Month 3**: Submit university pilot proposals. Begin free pilot at one university (if accepted). Instrument pilot fully: track every application submission, every suggestion given, every outcome (hired/not hired, employer name if disclosed).

## 5. B2B-Only Break-Even Path

### 5.1 Break-Even Math

**Monthly operating cost assumption** (lean Year 1, founder-led):
- Infrastructure (hosting, LLM costs): SGD 1,500/month
- Tooling + software: SGD 500/month
- Marketing + sales: SGD 1,000/month
- **Total lean burn**: SGD 3,000/month = SGD 36K/year

**Break-even point (B2B only)**:
- University Year 1 deal: SGD 15-30K (average: SGD 22K)
- Agency Year 1 deal: SGD 5K average (5 seats × SGD 10/seat/month × 10 months residual)

| Scenario | Deals Required | ARR |
|----------|---------------|-----|
| Agencies only | 7-8 agency deals at SGD 5K/year each | SGD 35-40K |
| Universities only | 1.5 university Year 1 deals (SGD 22K avg) | SGD 33K |
| Mixed (primary) | 2 university deals + 2 agency deals | SGD 44K + 10K = SGD 54K |

**Break-even conclusion**: 2 university Year 1 contracts (SGD 15-30K each) + 2-3 agency deals = SGD 50-65K ARR. This covers lean operating costs AND provides 6-12 months of runway.

### 5.2 The 18-Month Break-Even Scenario

| Month | Agency Deals | University | B2B ARR | Monthly Revenue | Status |
|-------|-------------|------------|---------|----------------|--------|
| 1-3 | 0-1 | 0 | SGD 0-5K | SGD 0-5K | Pre-revenue |
| 4-6 | 1-2 | 0 (pilot running) | SGD 10-15K | SGD 1-2K/month | Burning |
| 7-9 | 2-3 | 0-1 pilot close | SGD 25-40K | SGD 3-5K/month | Approaching |
| 10-12 | 3-5 | 1 Year 1 contract | SGD 50-70K | SGD 5-8K/month | Break-even |

**Realistic break-even**: Month 18-24 with 2-3 agency deals + 1 university Year 1 contract signed.

### 5.3 B2C Contribution to Break-Even

At 200-300 active Pro users (achievable by Month 9-12 at 4-6% conversion):
- Pro revenue: 250 users × SGD 19/month = SGD 4,750/month = SGD 57K/year
- This supplements B2B to reach break-even faster
- But B2B is the PRIMARY story — B2C provides resilience, not the plan

**The rule**: B2C covers baseline infrastructure cost. B2B covers growth and margin. Never plan B2C as the primary break-even path.
