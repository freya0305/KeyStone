## Executive Summary

**B2B is the primary revenue engine. B2C subscriptions are a data flywheel, not the path to break-even.**

Key findings:
- Agency deals close in **2-4 weeks**, contributing **SGD 1-5K/year each**
- University contracts take **18-24 months** to first paid contract
- Angel investment fills the **18-24 month gap** between launch and break-even
- **SGD 300-500K** pre-seed raise covers burn through break-even at Month 18-24
- At SGD 2-4M pre-seed valuation, founders dilute **10-18%** to raise SGD 300-500K

## 1. B2B Revenue Growth Velocity

### 1.1 Agency Partnerships — The Fast B2B Channel

**Why agencies are the priority B2B channel:**

| Factor | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Decision speed | Days to weeks (owner/director, no procurement) |
| Deal size | SGD 600-1,200/year per agency (5-10 recruiters × SGD 10-15/seat/month) |
| Sales cycle | 2-4 weeks from first conversation to signed |
| No tender required | Below SGD 30K threshold — director-level approval |
| Value prop | "Your candidates prepare better → higher offer acceptance → you earn placement fees faster" |

**Founder outreach capacity:**

| Month | Cold Outreach | Conversations | Deals Closed (4% rate) | Cumulative Deals |
|-------|-------------|--------------|----------------------|----------------|
| 1 | 20 | 15 | 0.6 | 0-1 |
| 2 | 25 | 18 | 0.7 | 1-2 |
| 3 | 30 | 22 | 0.9 | 2-3 |
| 4-6 | 30/month | 22/month | 0.9/month | 5-6 |
| 7-12 | 25/month | 18/month | 0.7/month | 9-12 |

**Agency deal economics:**

| Deal Size | Recruiters | Annual Revenue | 3-Year LTV |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Small agency | 5 | SGD 600 | SGD 1,800 |
| Medium agency | 10 | SGD 1,200 | SGD 3,600 |
| Large agency | 20 | SGD 2,400 | SGD 7,200 |

**Year 1 agency ARR projection:**

| Scenario | Deals Closed | ARR | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-----|-------|
| Low | 5 | SGD 6,000 | Conservative start |
| Mid | 8 | SGD 9,600 | Based on 150 outreach attempts + 4% close |
| High | 12 | SGD 14,400 | Strong outreach + 5% close rate |

**Key insight**: Agency deals are the **cash flow bridge** for Months 1-12. They close fast enough to contribute meaningful ARR before universities are even through their pilot phase.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations

### 4.1 Recommended Raise: SGD 300-500K

**Rationale**:
- SGD 300K is sufficient to reach break-even at Month 18-24
- SGD 500K provides buffer for university procurement delays
- Both scenarios keep founder dilution below 15%
- Startup SG Tech co-investment can double effective raise

**Use of funds**:
1. Founder living expenses through break-even
2. Minimal operating costs (lean B2C SaaS)
3. LLM inference at scale
4. Marketing for B2C acquisition (referral program + career coach partnerships)

### 4.2 B2B Acquisition Priority

| Priority | Channel | Timeline | Investment |
|----------|---------|----------|------------|
| **P1** | Agency partnerships | Month 1-12 | 30% of B2B time |
| **P2** | University pilots (free) | Month 1-6 | 20% of B2B time |
| **P3** | University conversion | Month 12-24 | 30% of B2B time |
| **P4** | WSG/government | Month 18+ | 20% of B2B time |

### 4.3 B2C Strategy Under New Paradigm

**B2C is NOT the revenue primary. B2C is:**

| Purpose | How |
|---------|-----|
| Data collection | Outcome logging builds moat |
| User research | Interview prep features validated here |
| Referral engine | Happy users refer agencies |
| University pipeline | Free users → university career centers |

**B2C pricing stays the same** (SGD 9 Basic, SGD 12 Pro). Conversion rate target: 4-5%.

## 5. For Discussion

1. **Is SGD 300K enough, or should we target SGD 500K for the university delay buffer?**

2. **Should we apply for Startup SG Tech immediately?** The 1:1 co-investment match (up to SGD 250K) effectively doubles every angel dollar. But the application takes 6-12 weeks.

3. **University pilots — free or paid?** Free pilots build outcome data and institutional credibility faster. But they delay revenue. Is the data moat worth more than the immediate SGD 15-30K?

4. **When to raise the seed round?** After Year 1 traction (200+ Pro users, 1-2 university pilots with data, 5+ agency deals), a SGD 1-2M seed at SGD 6-10M pre-money becomes possible. Should we plan for this explicitly?
